## 3. Support, Warranty & RMA Policy

### Support Channels
1. **Warranty & RMA Process** — return/repair authorization for products under warranty.
2. **Support Ticket** — raise and track service requests via the ticketing portal.
3. **Download Technical Resources** — drivers, SDKs, manuals, firmware (via download.mantratecapp.com).

Support services include: online software/hardware support, on-site support, repair & replacement, project services, manpower services, and SME installation support.

### Warranty Policy
- **Standard warranty:** One-year limited warranty on all Mantra products from date of purchase.
- **Repair timeline (in-warranty):** 4–5 business days.
- **Extended warranty:** Available on request; customers should contact their sales representative.
- **Scope:** Limited warranty applies only to physical goods purchased directly from Mantra Softech. Mantra is not responsible for warranty commitments made independently between its distributors and end customers.

### What's Covered Under Warranty
- Free repair and service for in-warranty devices (no charge for labor).
- Free component/parts replacement for faulty parts during the warranty period.
- Expert handling by qualified technicians using current diagnostic tools.
- Return shipping (Mantra-to-customer) covered by Mantra after repair.

### Shipping Responsibility
- Customer pays shipping cost when sending the device **to** Mantra.
- If a warranty breach is found on inspection, repair becomes chargeable.
- For valid RMAs where the originally reported issue is confirmed, Mantra covers shipping **both ways**.

### Non-Warranty (Out-of-Coverage) Repair Process
1. **Assessment** — Device received at the repair center (from HO or store). Fault assessment takes 10–12 business days. Inspection/service charges and component charges apply.
2. **Approval & Cost** — Estimated repair cost is shared with the customer. If approved, repair proceeds and an invoice is raised for service + parts (freight charges borne by customer). If not approved, device is returned unrepaired.
3. **Return & Dispatch** — After repair (or rejection), device is dispatched back. Mantra bears the dispatch cost if the device is returned without repair. For physically damaged goods, only transportation charges apply — no repair liability is taken by Mantra.

### RMA (Return Material Authorization) Journey — Step by Step
1. **Form Submission** — Branch/client fills the RMA form (device details, warranty status, reported issue) and emails it to servico@mantratec.com.
2. **Assignment & Transfer** — Head Office assigns an RMA number, acknowledges receipt, and forwards the device to the stores team for assessment/repair.
3. **Repair & Notification** — Stores team notifies HO once repair is complete; the dispatch team is alerted. Non-warranty devices are held pending payment confirmation.
4. **Dispatch & Tracking** — Customer receives courier docket details and shipment status updates from HO/Dispatch until delivery.

### Servico (Mantra's Service Support Division)
- **Email:** servico@mantratec.com
- **Phone:** +91-079-49068000
- **Website:** servico.mantratecapp.com
- Provides real-time maintenance and repair support for biometric devices and security systems.
