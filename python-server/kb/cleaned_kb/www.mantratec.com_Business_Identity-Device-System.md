## Fast start checklist

### How to plan the right biometric device deployment.

Before selecting biometric terminals, kits or kiosks, define the identity journey, operating environment, user volume, modality requirements and support model.

01

###### Define the identity journey

Separate enrollment, verification, authentication, self-service, border, SIM, banking and citizen service workflows.

02

###### Select the device type

Choose rugged terminal, enrollment kit, biometric POS or self-service kiosk based on the use case.

03

###### Map biometric modalities

Confirm fingerprint, face, iris, card, document, QR or POS requirements before final hardware selection.

04

###### Validate field conditions

Test network, power, mobility, dust, temperature, handling, battery and user flow requirements.

05

###### Plan integration

Connect devices to identity platforms, enrollment software, border systems, banking apps or government databases.

06

###### Define support and lifecycle

Plan warranty, firmware, spares, repair, training and long-term operational support before rollout.
