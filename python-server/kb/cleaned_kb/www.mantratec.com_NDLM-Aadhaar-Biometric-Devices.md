## FAQ: Questions on NDLM Biometric Devices

Common questions about using Mantra biometric devices for National Digital Literacy Mission operations.

### What is NDLM and its purpose?

NDLM (National Digital Literacy Mission) is a Central Government initiative to provide basic IT training to rural Indians - teaching them to use computers, the internet, digital payment platforms and electronic devices. The goal is to bridge the urban-rural digital divide and empower rural citizens to participate in the digital economy.

### Why is biometric registration needed for NDLM?

Biometric registration ensures genuine beneficiaries are enrolled and that attendance records accurately reflect real participation. This prevents ghost enrolments, ensures training stipends and certificates reach actual participants, and maintains data integrity for government audits of the NDLM programme.

### Can Mantra devices work in remote rural areas with poor connectivity?

Yes. Mantra MFS110 and MIS100V2 connect via USB and store biometric templates locally during authentication. While online UIDAI authentication requires internet, the devices support offline modes through UIDAI-certified software - ensuring functionality even in areas with intermittent connectivity.

### What documents can beneficiaries access through NDLM training?

NDLM training teaches beneficiaries to access DigiLocker for digital documents, use UPI/BHIM for digital payments, access government portals (UMANG, MyGov), make online utility payments, and use internet banking - all of which are enhanced by Aadhaar biometric authentication through Mantra devices.
