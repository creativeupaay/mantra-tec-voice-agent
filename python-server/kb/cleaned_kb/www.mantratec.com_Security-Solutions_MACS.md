# MACS: The Unified Access Control Platform

A comprehensive, unified platform combining identity onboarding, physical access control, visitor management, canteen operations, time and attendance, vehicle access, and AI face analytics - all in a single system built for enterprise and government environments.

## Why MACS

### Built for Enterprises That Demand Unified Control

MACS brings together every access, identity, and operations module under one platform - eliminating the integration overhead, data silos, and vendor sprawl that come with multi-product security deployments.

*   **Enhanced Security:** Ensures controlled access with multiple biometric authentication methods - card, face, fingerprint, and QR code - across all entry and exit points.
*   **Scalable & Customisable:** Adapts to the needs of small businesses and large enterprises alike. Multi-company and multi-location support with entity-level access profiles.
*   **Cloud & On-Premise Deployment:** Supports both cloud and on-premise options - including client cloud and Mantra-managed cloud - giving enterprises full control over their data residency.
*   **Seamless Integration:** Works with existing security infrastructure and enterprise applications - HR systems, ERP, CCTV, and payroll - via REST API and standard connectors.

## Platform Modules

### Multiple Modules. One Unified Platform.

Every MACS module works standalone or in combination - sharing device connections, employee profiles, and reporting infrastructure across the entire platform.

### Module 01: Identity Onboarding Gateway (IOG)

Unified enrollment and identity management across all MACS modules - card, face, fingerprint, and QR authentication configured from one dashboard with full card lifecycle control.

*   Card, Face, Fingerprint, QR auth
*   Real-time device health alerts
*   Temporary pass with approval flow
*   Card activation, PIN & revocation
*   Custom card design per entity
*   Multi-company profile management

### Module 02: Access Control System (ACS)

Manage and monitor access to buildings, restricted zones, and sensitive areas with real-time visibility, granular role-based control, and a full audit trail for every entry and exit event.

*   Configurable door & zone permissions
*   Punch records & failed attempt logs
*   ERT dashboard for emergencies
*   Gate pass real-time status board
*   Blacklist events & movement trails
*   Role-based access per profile

### Module 03: Visitor Management System (VMS)

Full visitor lifecycle management - from employee pre-registration and digital QR passes to contactless check-in, real-time tracking, badge printing, and compliance-ready audit reports.

*   Configurable request & approval workflow
*   Instant badge printing from dashboard
*   Blacklist across all locations
*   Visitor card lifecycle management
*   Analytics by location, device, dept
*   Court-admissible audit reports

### Module 04: Canteen Management System (CMS)

Centralised corporate dining management with real-time meal tracking, device-level consumption monitoring, flexible subsidy control, and automated financial reconciliation across multiple locations.

*   Device-wise punch IN/OUT tracking
*   Flexible primary & subsidiary meals
*   Real-time consumption monitoring
*   Subsidy by entity, meal & location
*   Financial reconciliation reports
*   Cashless & prepaid wallet payments

### Module 05: Time & Attendance (T&A)

Biometric attendance with automated payroll integration, shift and leave management, geo-fencing for field staff, and auto-generated statutory compliance reports for PF, ESI, and more.

*   Shift scheduling & OT calculations
*   Leave with accrual & encashment
*   Auto PF, ESI & statutory forms
*   Employee mobile self-service app
*   Geo-fence for field & remote staff
*   ERP & payroll integration ready

### Module 06: Face Mood Analytics (FMA)

AI-powered facial analysis for employee sentiment and behavioural insights at access points - enabling proactive HR interventions and security awareness without storing any raw biometric images.

*   Real-time mood & sentiment detection
*   Trend dashboards for HR teams
*   Security-relevant anomaly flagging
*   Privacy-compliant - no image storage
*   Linked to attendance & access events
*   Configurable alert thresholds

### Module 07: Vehicle Access Permit (VAP)

Automated vehicle identification and permit management for enterprise campuses, industrial estates, and government compounds - with RFID, camera-based recognition, and a full entry-exit audit trail.

*   RFID & camera-based vehicle ID
*   Permit issuance, renewal & revocation
*   Blacklist with real-time gate alerts
*   Parking bay & occupancy tracking
*   Complete entry-exit audit trail

## FAQ

### Frequently Asked Questions

What enterprise decision-makers ask before deploying MACS.

### Can MACS modules be deployed individually?

Every MACS module is designed to operate independently - you can start with just Access Control or just Visitor Management, and add modules over time as your requirements evolve. When multiple modules are active, they share a common employee database, device management layer, and reporting infrastructure.

### Does MACS work with our existing biometric devices?

MACS is designed for compatibility with a broad range of biometric hardware including fingerprint readers, face recognition terminals, RFID card readers, and QR code scanners. A site survey is included to identify which devices can be retained.

### How long does a MACS deployment take?

A single-site deployment typically goes live within 3 to 5 weeks, including hardware installation, software configuration, employee data migration, enrollment, and user training.

### Can MACS integrate with our SAP or Oracle HR system?

Yes. MACS provides RESTful API integration with SAP HR, SAP S/4HANA HCM, Oracle HCM Cloud, Oracle E-Business Suite, and major Indian HR platforms including Keka and GreytHR.

### What support is available after deployment?

Mantra offers Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) tailored to each scale. Standard AMC includes quarterly preventive maintenance, remote monitoring, and next-business-day response.
