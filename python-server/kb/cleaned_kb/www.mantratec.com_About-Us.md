## Pioneering Excellence from startup to global leader

Helping millions around the world manage their digital identity requirements.

**2006**

###### Company Incorporation

Mantra Softech India Private Limited officially incorporated, marking the beginning of India's biometric innovation journey.

**2008**

###### Market Validation

Briefly imported and sold biometric devices to understand market dynamics and develop customer-centric solutions.

**2010**

###### Indigenous Innovation

Successfully developed proprietary fingerprint sensor and algorithm with embedded software, achieving technological self-reliance in biometrics.

**2012**

###### STQC Certification

Received prestigious STQC Certificate from UIDAI for Aadhaar program, becoming trusted partner in national identity.

**2014**

###### FBI Recognition

Achieved FBI certification for fingerprint scanner, validating world-class quality standards and international compliance capabilities.

**2016**

###### IRIS Innovation

Developed advanced IRIS sensor for eye authentication and launched mobile fingerprint and IRIS systems.

**2018**

###### Brand Expansion

Launched RF Energie for RFID solutions alongside Servico, MINOP, and Mivanta, diversifying technology portfolio significantly.

**2019**

###### Strategic Brands

Introduced Merepos and Moreze brands, creating specialized market segments for POS terminals and enterprise security solutions.

**2021**

###### R&D Recognition

R&D unit officially recognized by Ministry of Science and Technology for groundbreaking contributions in biometric technology.

**2022**

###### Patent Achievement

Received Technology Patent Certification for innovative Optical Fingerprint Scanner, protecting proprietary intellectual property and innovation leadership.

**2023**

###### NIST Listing

Mantra Softech officially listed on NIST for both Face and Fingerprint recognition, achieving global benchmarking.

**2025**

###### World First

Achieved world's first Dual IRIS iBeta certificate, establishing new global standards in biometric liveness detection.
