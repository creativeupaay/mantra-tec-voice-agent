## RFID Solutions Built for Purpose

Every solution runs on our own RFID hardware - purpose-built readers, tamper-proof tags, and cloud dashboards designed to work together.

### Inventory Management

Track stock levels and movement across multiple warehouses in real time. Cut manual counting cycles and shrinkage losses.

### Asset Management

Know where every tagged asset is - from IT equipment to factory tools. Get automatic alerts when assets leave a zone.

### Vehicle Access Control

Automate entry and exit for authorized vehicles at plant gates and parking bays. Integrates with boom barriers.

### Warehouse Automation

Speed up goods receive, picking, and dispatch with hands-free RFID scanning. Connects to your existing WMS or ERP.

### Parking Management

Manage parking access, occupancy tracking, and permit verification using long-range UHF readers.

### Attendance Management

Record entry and exit times for employees or students using RFID cards. Exports directly to payroll systems.

### Smart City Management

Track municipal assets and waste collection vehicles across city zones with GPS and RFID combined.

### Automated Armory

Manage weapons and equipment issuance for defense organizations with RFID-controlled cabinets and biometric access.
