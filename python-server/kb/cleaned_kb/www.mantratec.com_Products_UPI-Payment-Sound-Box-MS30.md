## Intelligent Cloud Reader with Dynamic QR Code

MS30 goes beyond standard soundboxes with a dynamic QR code display, 2.4-inch color customer screen, and customizable real-time audio alerts - giving merchants complete payment visibility.

### Dynamic QR Display

Intelligent Cloud Reader

Generates a unique, transaction-specific QR code for every payment. This eliminates static QR fraud risks and ensures every payment is correctly attributed to the right merchant and amount automatically.

### 2.4" Color Screen

TFT | 320\*240px | 6-Digit LED

High-resolution TFT color screen gives customers visual confirmation of their transaction. Complemented by a 6-digit LED display for the merchant and status indicators for battery and network connectivity.

### 85dB Voice Alert

4 Ohm | 3W | Adjustable

Announces exact transaction amounts in real time. Audio alerts are fully customizable, allowing merchants to set personalized confirmation messages. Adjustable volume suits everything from quiet boutiques to loud market stalls.

### 4G LTE + Wi-Fi

LTE-FDD/TDD | optional Wi-Fi 2.4GHz

Primary 4G LTE and 2G EGSM fallback with optional Wi-Fi 2.4GHz connectivity - ensuring reliable payment notifications even in areas with inconsistent cellular coverage.

### OTA Updates

DFOTA | NTP Time Sync | Remote

Over-the-air updates for firmware (DFOTA) and audio notification files. Network operators can push new announcement tones, languages, or security patches without physical device access. NTP ensures accurate timestamps.

### Enterprise Compliance

BIS | CE | WPC | RoHS

Certified to highest hardware standards. ESD protection: 8kV contact and 15kV air discharge. Operates reliably from 0oC to 50oC in high-humidity environments.
