## ABOUT AePS

### Banking at Your Doorstep with Aadhaar Fingerprint

AePS (Aadhaar Enabled Payment System) is an NPCI-developed payment service that uses the Aadhaar identity infrastructure to enable banking services at the last mile - through Business Correspondents (BCs) equipped with a Micro ATM (a POS-like device) and a biometric scanner.

Micro ATMs work similarly to a traditional ATM - offering cash withdrawal, cash deposit, balance enquiry, mini statement and inter-bank fund transfers - but require no debit card or PIN. Instead, customers authenticate using their Aadhaar-linked bank account via a fingerprint scan. This is transformational for rural India where traditional ATMs are scarce and banking penetration is low.

Mantra's MARC10 / MARC11 Micro ATM devices and MFS110 fingerprint scanners are deployed by leading banks and MFIs for AePS banking correspondent operations - bringing formal financial services to unbanked populations.

## AePS TRANSACTION FLOW

### How AePS Micro ATM Transaction Works

1.  **Customer Presents Aadhaar**
    The customer provides their Aadhaar number (UID) and selects the transaction type - cash withdrawal, balance check, mini statement or fund transfer.
2.  **Biometric Authentication**
    Customer places their fingerprint on the Mantra scanner connected to the BC's Micro ATM. UIDAI authenticates the identity through the encrypted L1 RD Service.
3.  **Transaction & Settlement**
    On successful authentication, the transaction is processed through NPCI's AePS switch - cash is dispensed by the BC and the customer's Aadhaar-linked account is debited immediately.

## FAQ

### Questions on AePS Micro ATM Biometrics

Answers to common queries about setting up and operating AePS banking correspondent services with Mantra biometrics.

*   **What is AePS (Aadhaar Enabled Payment System)?**
    AePS is an NPCI-developed banking payment system that uses Aadhaar biometric authentication to allow individuals to access their bank accounts - irrespective of which bank they hold the account with - through a banking correspondent equipped with a Micro ATM and biometric scanner. Services include cash withdrawal, balance inquiry, mini statement and fund transfer.
*   **Does the customer need to know their account number for AePS?**
    No. The customer only needs to remember their Aadhaar number (12 digits) and provide their fingerprint. The AePS system uses their Aadhaar-linked bank account - there is no need to know the account number, IFSC code, PIN or password.
*   **Which banks support AePS transactions?**
    AePS works with accounts at over 100 scheduled commercial banks in India that have seeded their customers' Aadhaar numbers - including SBI, PNB, BOI, BOB, Canara Bank, Axis, HDFC, ICICI, IDBI, UCO Bank and all major RRBs (Regional Rural Banks) and cooperative banks.

    Yes. Mantra MFS110 supports the UIDAI L1 RD Service API which is the standard interface for all AePS applications from banks, fintech companies, NPCI-approved BCs and Microfinance Institutions. No custom integration is required - it works with all AePS apps on Android and Windows out of the box.
