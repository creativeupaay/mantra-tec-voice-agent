## ABOUT THE SYSTEM
### Real-time, Location-verified Government Employee Attendance

The Aadhaar Based GPS Biometric Attendance System is a Government of India initiative under the Digital India programme to record attendance of Central Government employees through the attendance.gov.in portal. Employees mark their attendance via Aadhaar-authenticated fingerprint or iris scan on biometric devices installed at government offices - with attendance data instantly uploaded to the central NIC server.

The GPS tagging component adds an additional layer of authenticity - ensuring attendance is marked from the designated office location and not remotely. This is particularly important for field-level government officers who visit sites. The system provides the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), ministries and departments with real-time attendance dashboards, late-coming reports and absence analytics.

Mantra's UIDAI-approved biometric devices are deployed across Central Government ministries, state government offices, PSUs, autonomous bodies and educational institutions implementing AEBAS - serving lakhs of government employees daily.

## HOW IT WORKS
### AEBAS Attendance Process Flow

1. Biometric Authentication
   The employee places their finger on the Mantra biometric device. The system authenticates via UIDAI's encrypted L1 RD Service, confirming identity in under 2 seconds.

2. GPS Location Capture
   Simultaneously, the device (or connected app) captures the GPS coordinates of the location - ensuring the employee is physically present at the registered office address.

3. Real-time Data Upload
   Attendance timestamp, biometric verification status and GPS coordinates are instantly uploaded to attendance.gov.in - accessible to department heads and DoPT in real time.

## FAQ
### Questions on AEBAS GPS Attendance

Answers about implementing Aadhaar-based GPS biometric attendance systems for government departments.

###### What is AEBAS and the attendance.gov.in portal?

AEBAS (Aadhaar Enabled Biometric Attendance System) is a Central Government initiative mandating biometric attendance for all Central Government employees. The attendance.gov.in portal (managed by NIC) records and displays attendance data in real time - accessible to DoPT, department heads and ministries for monitoring employee punctuality and absenteeism.

###### Is Mantra MFS110 compatible with attendance.gov.in?

Yes. Mantra MFS110 supports UIDAI L1 RD Service and is fully compatible with the attendance.gov.in application and all AEBAS-compliant attendance management software. The device works on Windows desktops used in government offices without any additional configuration.

###### How does GPS tagging work with biometric attendance?

In mobile attendance apps (AEBAS Android app), GPS coordinates are captured simultaneously with the biometric authentication. The system verifies that the employee's current GPS location matches the registered office geofence - ensuring attendance is marked from the workplace, not from a remote location.

###### Can AEBAS be deployed for state government departments too?

Yes. While AEBAS was initially mandated for Central Government employees, many state governments have implemented their own Aadhaar-based biometric attendance systems. Mantra MFS110 and MIS100V2 are deployed in state government offices across multiple states - including MoU-based implementations through state IT departments.
