## How It Works

A simple, streamlined five-step process to get your visitor management system running efficiently.

*   **Visitor Arrival**
    Visitors check in using Monuk's touchscreen interface or mobile app.
*   **Verification**
    The system captures a photo and verifies the visitor's ID instantly.
*   **Notification**
    The host is instantly notified of their visitor's arrival via email or SMS.
*   **Check-In**
    Visitors receive a custom badge and proceed to their meeting site.
*   **Check-Out**
    Visitors check out and the visitor log is updated in real time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Monuk Visitor Management System.

*   **Does Monuk support pre-registration for guests?**
    Yes, guests can be pre-registered via a web portal or mobile app to reduce wait times at the reception. This allows for a smoother check-in process upon arrival.
*   **Can we blacklist specific individuals from entering?**
    Yes, Monuk includes a robust whitelist and blacklist feature. Security personnel are instantly alerted if a blacklisted individual attempts to register for entry.
*   **Does Monuk support badge printing and QR codes?**
    Absolutely. Monuk can generate e-passes and print physical badges with QR codes, which can then be scanned at various access points within your facility.
*   **Can Monuk notify hosts when their visitor arrives?**
    Yes, hosts receive instant notifications via E-mail or SMS the moment their guest completes the registration process at the kiosk or reception desk.
*   **Is Monuk compatible with our existing access control system?**
    Monuk is designed for seamless integration with major Access Control Systems, allowing you to automatically assign physical access rights based on predefined visitor groups.
