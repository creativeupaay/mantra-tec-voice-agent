## FAQ

### Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our workforce management solutions.

###### How does your workforce management system handle contract labor?

Our system is specifically designed to manage both permanent and temporary workers. It simplifies contract labor management by tracking attendance, calculating wages based on shifts, and ensuring that only authorized personnel can enter specific zones.

###### Can the system prevent unauthorized access to specific industrial zones?

Yes, the system allows for zonal restrictions. You can define access permissions based on worker roles or shifts, ensuring that only registered and authorized workers can enter restricted premises or plants.

###### Does it support automatic wage calculation for different shifts?

Absolutely. The system records clock-in and clock-out times and can automatically calculate daily, weekly, or monthly wages, including overtime and double shift records, reducing administrative overhead and errors.

###### What is the MABIS de-duplication technology?

MABIS (Mantra Automated Biometric Identification System) is a powerful engine that prevents multiple enrollments by the same individual. It ensures data integrity by matching biometric data against existing records during registration.

###### How does the system track real-time headcounts?

The system provides a real-time dashboard for headcount of both personnel and vehicles. It can bifurcate data based on entities like office staff, contractors, visitors, and drivers, giving you a clear view of everyone on the premises.

###### Can your employee tracking software integrate with our current HRMS?

Yes. Our biometric solutions integrate seamlessly with your existing HRMS to enable smart payroll integration. This automates faultless payroll calculations and provides real-time analytics on employee hours, shift adherence, and overtime.

###### Does your facial recognition technology work if employees are wearing masks?

Absolutely. Terminals like our AI-powered BioNIC F7 are optimized for mask detection using adaptive AI. They feature fast, sub-second matching speeds and utilize live-body detection to accurately identify employees and prevent photo spoofing.
