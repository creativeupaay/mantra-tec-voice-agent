## Mantra's Computer Vision Solutions

Every Mantra solution is designed for the conditions of actual use - not lab conditions. Our computer vision models are trained on real-world data at scale, and our hardware is manufactured in-house with zero tolerance for inconsistency.

### Facial Recognition

Mantra's facial recognition technology does not just match a face. It confirms that the face is real. Our AI-powered liveness detection - built on deep learning models trained for texture, depth, and reflectance analysis - flags presentation attacks using photos, videos, or masks before authentication is accepted. This is the technology UIDAI cited when awarding Mantra the Certificate of Recognition for Outstanding Contribution to Innovation in Biometric Authentication.

In production today: government employee attendance under the AEBAS program, contactless access control for enterprise campuses, and remote customer onboarding for banking and fintech platforms. Our systems operate in daylight, low light, and infrared - indoors and outdoors.

### Fingerprint Scanning

Mantra's fingerprint scanners are FBI-certified, STQC-approved, and Aadhaar-compliant. They are built for the conditions that matter in large-scale deployments: outdoor banking kiosks in direct sunlight, welfare beneficiaries with worn or aged fingerprints, and high-throughput attendance gates running thousands of scans daily.

Our US-patented OCT-based optical scanner goes further than any surface-level sensor - it reads both external and internal finger structures simultaneously, so a silicone mold or printed replica simply does not pass. This technology was developed entirely in-house, is manufactured in India, and is protected by US Patent No. US 12,315,287 B2. Our anti-spoofing AI model, ASIM, extends this protection across fingerprint, iris, and face modalities in a single unified system - a world first.

### Iris Scanning

Mantra's MATISX dual iris scanner is the world's first dual-iris device to clear iBeta Level-1 PAD certification - achieving a 0% APCER (Attack Presentation Classification Error Rate) against Level-1 attacks under ISO/IEC 30107-3. In plain terms: it scored a perfect pass against every liveness attack thrown at it during testing.

The MATISX captures both eyes simultaneously using near-infrared imaging, works through standard glasses and contact lenses, and is deployed across national ID programs, border control installations, and welfare distribution systems in India, Asia, Africa, and the Middle East. Mantra also holds India's first iBeta iris certification - and remains the only Indian company to have achieved it.

### Multimodal Biometrics

No single modality covers every risk. Mantra's integrated solutions combine fingerprint, iris, face, and behavioral signals - keystroke dynamics, gait analysis, and interaction patterns - into a single authentication framework that continuously checks identity throughout a session, not just at the point of entry.

This matters most in financial services, where account takeover happens after a legitimate login. Our multimodal deployments give fraud teams the ability to catch impersonation mid-session - before a transaction clears. Mantra has also filed plans to develop an advanced integrated system combining fingerprint and behavioral biometrics, bringing physical and continuous identity verification together in one platform.
