# Attendance System

Mantra’s attendance management provides you with a practical solution to reduce processing of employee time sheets and decrease human errors associated with manual time keeping process. It not only improves personal efficiency but also results in saving time & money both and undoubtedly, it enhances transparency in the system and further assists in building trusts in all level of engagements.

Attendance system design and structure supports quick and effective integration with ERP’s and HRMS solutions including SAP & Oracle and integral elements of corporate system strategy and execution.

The system provides end-to-end automation of tracking, managing and scheduling employee hours in a seamless manner with the interoperable Bio-metric technology and hardware controllers.
