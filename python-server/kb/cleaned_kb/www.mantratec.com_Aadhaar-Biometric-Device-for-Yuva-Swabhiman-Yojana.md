## How Biometric eKYC Works in Yuva Swabhiman Yojana

1.  **eKYC at Nodal Office**
    Every nodal office carries out the eKYC process of the beneficiaries through an Aadhaar-enabled biometric device - verifying identity against the UIDAI database in real-time.

2.  **Skill Mapping & Supervisor Assignment**
    After eKYC completion, the nodal office assigns the beneficiary a supervisor based on their skills for the 10-day skill development training programme.

3.  **Training & Employment Deployment**
    Beneficiaries receive 10 days of field-based skill training followed by 90 days of paid employment (4 hours/day). SMS notifications keep beneficiaries, nodal officers and supervisors updated throughout.
