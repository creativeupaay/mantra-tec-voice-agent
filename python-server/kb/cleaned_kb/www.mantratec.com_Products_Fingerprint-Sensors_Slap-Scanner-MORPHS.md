## Technical Details

Comprehensive technical specifications for system integrators and procurement teams.

### Sensor & Image

*   **Sensor Type:** Optical Scratch Free Sensor
*   **Pixel Resolution:** 500 DPI +/- 1%
*   **Platen Area:** 3.5 x 3.2 in. (90 x 81 mm)
*   **Active Platen Area:** 3.2 x 3.0 in. (81.2 x 76.2 mm)
*   **Finger Image Size:** 1600 x 1500 Pixels
*   **Pixel Depth:** 8 Bits/Pixel (256 Gray Level)(size 1 byte)
*   **Capturing Speed:** 4 FPS Continuous image capture\*
*   **Capture Mode:** Auto capture with built-in quality check (incorporates NIST quality considerations)
*   **Rolled Capture Area:** 1.6 x 1.5 Inch (40.64 x 38.01 mm)
*   **Rolled Capture Speed:** 10 FPS Continuous image capture\*
*   **Rolled Capture Image Size:** 800 x 750 Pixels
*   **Live Finger Detection (LFD):** ISO 30107-3 Compliant
*   **Signal to Noise Ratio:** Both White & Black SNR >=125
*   **Platen Area Hardness:** 9H compliant Tested as per ASTM D3363
*   **Platen Area Abrasion:** 175g, 400 cycles Abrasion test compliant as per ASTM F 2357
*   **Capturing Format:** Flat Four fingers, Two thumbs and Rolled fingerprints
*   **Biometric Data Interchange Format:** ISO/IEC 19794-4:2005 & 2011 for Image Template, WSQ3.1, JPEG2000

### Hardware

*   **Interface:** USB 2.0 (High speed), USB-IF Certified
*   **Operating Voltage:** 5V +/- 5% (USB Powered Device)
*   **Current Consumption:** Operating: < 350 mA (Max) & Stand By: < 80 mA (Max)
*   **Operating Temperature:** 0 to 50oC
*   **Operating Humidity:** 10-90% RH, non-condensing
*   **ESD Protection:** Contact Discharge: +/- 4kV, Air Discharge: +/- 8kV
*   **LED Indicators:** Power, Status, and fingerprint image quality
*   **Dimensions:** 173 x 147 x 121 mm
*   **Weight:** 3.6 lbs (1.6 kg)
