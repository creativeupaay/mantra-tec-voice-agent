## Fast start checklist: How to plan the right identity solution.

Before selecting a digital identity platform, define the user journey, regulatory environment, data sources, integration model and assurance level.

01. **Define the identity journey**: Separate enrolment, verification, authentication, credential sharing, signing and continuous assurance use cases.
02. **Map the population and risk**: Identify citizen, customer, employee or beneficiary volumes along with fraud and compliance exposure.
03. **Select the standards layer**: Plan MOSIP, W3C VC, OAuth, OpenID Connect, FIDO2, WebAuthn, ISO or NIST alignment early.
04. **Integrate data sources**: Connect national ID APIs, civil registries, payment rails, AML databases, HR systems or credential issuers.
05. **Build consent and audit**: Design consent, attribute release, audit logging, case review and reporting controls before rollout.
06. **Validate at scale**: Test throughput, liveness, deduplication, edge cases, network conditions and operational support before mass deployment.
