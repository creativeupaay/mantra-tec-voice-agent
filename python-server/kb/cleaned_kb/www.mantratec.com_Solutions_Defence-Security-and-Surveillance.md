## Defence Security FAQ

### Frequently Asked Questions

Below are clear answers to common questions about our indigenous defence security platforms.

*   **What is DEPS and how does it secure defence perimeters?**
    DEPS - Defence Electronic Perimeter Security - is Mantra's purpose-built perimeter intrusion detection system for high-risk military and government sites. It combines intelligent sensor arrays, AI-based motion analytics, and real-time alert management to detect, classify, and report intrusion attempts along physical perimeters. The system is designed for harsh outdoor environments, supports 24/7 operations, and integrates directly with CCTV, lighting control, and command-room dashboards.
*   **What are MARS AI and IPAS AI, and how are they different?**
    MARS AI (Multi-modal AI Recognition System) is Mantra's intelligent analytics platform for real-time identification and threat classification using video and biometric inputs. IPAS AI (Intelligent Perimeter and Access Security) extends this into access enforcement - combining AI-driven surveillance with automated gate control, biometric verification, and permit management. Together, they provide end-to-end identity assurance from the perimeter to interior restricted zones.
*   **Is Mantra's defence security platform indigenously manufactured?**
    Yes. Mantra designs and manufactures its defence security platforms in India across a 1.5 lakh sq.ft. shop floor. All hardware - biometric terminals, RFID readers, sensor modules, and edge processing units - is developed under Make in India with in-house R&D and global technology patents. This ensures supply-chain security, customisation without vendor dependency, and alignment with government procurement preferences for critical infrastructure.
*   **How does Mantra's Logical Security and ANPR module work in a defence context?**
    The Logical Security module governs digital access to defence networks, command systems, and classified data environments - enforcing multi-factor authentication and role-based access control. The ANPR (Automatic Number Plate Recognition) layer handles vehicle movement at garrison gates, ammunition depots, and restricted entry points - identifying, logging, and controlling vehicle access in real time with tamper alerts and full audit trails.
*   **What Video Management and Border Security capabilities does Mantra offer?**
    Mantra's VMS (Video Management System) provides centralised recording, live monitoring, event-triggered playback, and AI-based video analytics across distributed camera networks at defence sites. For border security, the platform extends to perimeter surveillance, multi-sensor fusion, command-level situational awareness, and integration with ANPR and biometric checkpoints - supporting applications from forward operating bases to border fencing zones.
