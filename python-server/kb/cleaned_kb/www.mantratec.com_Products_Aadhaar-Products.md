# Aadhaar Products

Aadhaar is a biometric resident Identification program from Govt of India. It aims to empower the citizens of India with a unique identity by building a digital platform for the authentication of an individual at any time and any place. It helps to access Govt services related to various welfare schemes in Education, Banking, Health care, Agriculture, Insurance, Telecom and other sectors.
