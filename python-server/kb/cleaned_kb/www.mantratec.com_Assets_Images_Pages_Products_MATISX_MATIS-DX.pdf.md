## Dual IRIS Scanner MATIS DX

MATIS DX is a rugged, portable and easy-to-use dual-iris capture device that can simultaneously capture both irises with higher accuracy and greater speed. The innovative dual iris scanner is packed with compelling features that make the device suitable for diverse biometric enrollment and identification applications.
