## ISO Certifications

Mantra Softech holds five active ISO certifications covering quality management, information security, environmental compliance, IT service management, and occupational health & safety.

#### ISO 9001:2015

Affirms Mantra Softech's commitment to quality management excellence - consistently meeting customer requirements while fostering a culture of continuous improvement.

### IT Service Management

#### ISO 20000

Validates our structured approach to IT service delivery, ensuring consistent, high-quality technology services across all customer engagements and support operations.

### Environmental Management

#### ISO 14001

Demonstrates Mantra's structured approach to minimising environmental impact across manufacturing and operations, reflecting our commitment to sustainable business practices.

### Information Security

#### ISO 27001:2013

Highlights our superior information security practices - safeguarding client data, ensuring confidentiality, and maintaining the highest standards of risk management and cybersecurity.

### Occupational Health & Safety

#### ISO 45001:2018

Demonstrates our dedication to occupational health and safety - creating a safe and productive work environment, prioritising employee well-being, and adhering to global safety standards.
