## ABOUT VTP
## Aadhaar Biometrics for Vocational Skill Development

The Vocational Training Programme (VTP) is a Government of India skill development initiative conducted through Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs), Polytechnics and private vocational centres. VTP focuses on equipping youth with practical, trade-specific competencies that improve their employability in manufacturing, services and technology sectors.

Aadhaar-based biometric devices are mandated at VTP centres for accurate student enrolment (eKYC), daily attendance marking and post-training placement verification. Mantra's devices are trusted by VTP implementing agencies to maintain transparent, auditable records linked to the NCVT (National Council for Vocational Training) and other skill authority systems.

## FAQ
## Questions on VTP Biometrics

Common questions about biometric device requirements for Vocational Training Programme operations.

### What biometric device is required for VTP attendance?

VTP mandates UIDAI L1 certified Aadhaar biometric devices for student attendance and eKYC. Mantra MFS110 (fingerprint) and MIS100V2 (iris) are both approved - MFS110 for primary authentication and MIS100V2 as fallback for trainees with difficult fingerprints.

### How does biometric attendance improve VTP outcomes?

Biometric attendance eliminates proxy attendance (a common challenge in skill training), ensures certification is awarded only to genuinely trained candidates, and provides auditable records for government inspections and NCVT evaluations - directly improving placement rates and scheme credibility.

### Are Mantra devices compatible with NCVT-MIS portal?

Yes. Mantra devices integrate with the NCVT-MIS portal via standard UIDAI RD Service APIs. Any VTP centre software that uses Aadhaar L1 authentication can work with Mantra MFS110 or MIS100V2 out of the box.
