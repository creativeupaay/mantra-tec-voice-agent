## CORE TECHNOLOGY
### Mantra's Behavioral Signal Stack

Six signals. One risk score. Zero user interruption.

### Keystroke Dynamics

Mantra captures the timing between keystrokes, key hold duration, and error correction patterns. This alone is enough to distinguish a human user from a credential-stuffing bot with over 95% accuracy.

### Touch & Swipe Behavior

On mobile, how a user taps, scrolls, and swipes is as unique as a fingerprint. Mantra's mobile SDK captures pressure, angle, velocity, and contact area - signals that are nearly impossible to spoof programmatically.

### Mouse & Navigation Patterns

The speed, trajectory, and micro-corrections in mouse movement are individually characteristic. Bot-driven navigation moves in straight lines at machine speed. Mantra's engine spots the difference instantly.

### Voice Biometrics

For call center and IVR authentication, Mantra adds voice pattern analysis - capturing pitch, cadence, and speech rhythm to verify returning customers without a single security question.

### Gait & Motion Analysis

On mobile devices, the accelerometer and gyroscope data captured while a user walks with their phone creates a motion signature. Mantra uses this as a passive, always-on authentication signal for high-security mobile sessions.
