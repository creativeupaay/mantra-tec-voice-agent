## About CMCHIS

The Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (recognized as CMCHIS) aims to accommodate paperless and cashless treatment to the vulnerable families in hospitals through health insurance coverage benefit. The implementation of CMCHIS is vital for states to attain the goal of Universal Health Care. Under this program, the government of the corresponding state will extend the benefits of health insurance coverage to the eligible individual/families after carrying out their biometric verification through biometric devices.

## How to Enroll for CMCHIS

1.  ### Income Certification

    Acquire the income tax certification for the family from the respective village administrative officer (VOA).
2.  ### Document Submission

    A head/member of the family shall carry the original Ration Card (with a Xerox copy) and a income certificate to the nearest district kiosk.
3.  ### Biometric Capture

    The operator shall carry out the verification process of the documents, after which the member shall be enrolled by capturing biometric details along with his/her demographic details.
4.  ### E-card Generation

    After successful biometric verification and authentication, an E-card will be generated and issued to the beneficiary.
