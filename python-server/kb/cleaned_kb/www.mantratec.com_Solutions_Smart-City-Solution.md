## Traffic Management System

### Adaptive Traffic Control That Thinks in Real Time

Traditional fixed-timer traffic signals were designed for standard traffic patterns, not real-time congestion. Mantra’s sensor-based Traffic Management System continuously monitors live vehicle density at every junction and dynamically adjusts signal timings in real time to prevent bottlenecks before they occur. All junction data is connected to a centralised Command Centre, giving traffic authorities complete city-wide visibility and control through a single dashboard.

#### Live Junction Monitoring

Sensor data from every controlled intersection, refreshed every few seconds and pushed to the CCC dashboard.

#### Incident Detection and Alert

Automatic notifications to traffic wardens when sensor patterns flag a collision or unusual stoppage.

#### Historical Traffic Analytics

Weekly and monthly reports on peak-hour patterns, helping urban planners make infrastructure decisions.

## Smart Parking System

### Better Space Use, Less Waiting Around

Roughly 30% of urban congestion comes from drivers searching for parking. Designed for municipal parking authorities and private operators alike, the system supports online booking, digital payment, and automatic enforcement flagging when a vehicle overstays its slot.

## IoT-Enabled Solid Waste Management

### Fewer Missed Collections. Lower Fuel Bills.

Sending waste vehicles out on fixed routes regardless of bin fill levels wastes fuel, misses critical pickups, and ties up fleet capacity. Mantra's IoT-enabled system puts fill-level sensors in bins and GPS trackers on every vehicle, then uses that data to generate the shortest viable route each day.

Supervisors see fleet location, bin status, and route completion in real time. Citizens can raise missed-collection complaints directly through the integrated grievance portal, and supervisors get notified instantly.

#### GPS Fleet Tracking

Live vehicle location visible to supervisors and CCC operators - with trip history and idle-time reports.

#### Fill-Level Alerts

Bins send automated alerts when 80% full, so vehicles are dispatched before overflow happens.

#### How long does a full city-wide deployment take?

For most municipalities, end-to-end deployment - sensor installation, CCC setup, staff training, and go-live - completes within 6 months. Smaller pilot zones typically go live in 8 to 10 weeks.

Yes. The platform is built to meet Smart Cities Mission and framework requirements, including data residency, audit trails, and documentation standards that municipal and state-level tenders typically ask for.

All data is stored on government-grade servers with options for on-premise, private cloud, or hybrid deployment depending on your authority's policy. The municipality retains full data ownership at all times.

Yes. The platform has open APIs and has been integrated with existing GIS systems, police dispatch software, payment gateways, and citizen-facing city apps across multiple deployments.
