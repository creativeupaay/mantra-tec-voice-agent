## FAQ

### Frequently Asked Questions

What enterprise decision-makers ask before committing to a Mantra biometric deployment.

###### How long does a typical enterprise biometric deployment take from contract to go-live?

A single-site enterprise deployment - one office campus or one factory - typically goes from contract signing to full go-live in 3 to 6 weeks, including hardware installation, software configuration, ERP integration testing, staff enrollment, and user training. Multi-site enterprise rollouts are structured as phased programs: each site goes live independently, so the organisation begins realising value from Site 1 while Sites 2 and 3 are being configured. We provide a site-by-site deployment schedule as part of the proposal documentation.

###### Can Mantra biometric systems integrate with our existing SAP or Oracle ERP?

Yes. Mantra provides native integration adapters for SAP HR (ESS/MSS), SAP S/4HANA HCM, Oracle HCM Cloud, and Oracle E-Business Suite. The attendance data feed to SAP and Oracle can be configured for real-time sync or scheduled batch transfer depending on your system architecture. For custom ERP environments, Mantra's REST API delivers attendance events in JSON format that any competent SAP or Oracle developer can consume within a standard integration project. An integration feasibility assessment is included at no charge as part of the pre-sales process.

###### What support model is available after deployment?

Mantra offers Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) tailored to enterprise deployment scale. Standard AMC includes quarterly preventive maintenance visits, remote monitoring of device health, software update management, and next-business-day on-site response. Premium AMC (recommended for critical facilities) provides 4-hour on-site SLA for device failures and includes a swap unit program so a failed terminal is replaced without waiting for repair. Mantra's pan-India service network covers all major states and union territories through a combination of direct field engineers and authorised service partners.

###### How does biometric data privacy compliance work for enterprises?

Biometric data in India is classified as sensitive personal data under the IT Act and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023. Mantra's deployment architecture stores biometric templates exclusively on-premise - on the device itself or on your organisation's server - with AES-256 encryption. Templates are never transmitted to or stored on Mantra's servers or any third-party cloud. This on-premise architecture keeps your organisation fully compliant with DPDPA requirements and eliminates the data processing agreements complexity that cloud-based biometric storage would require. We provide standard employee consent documentation templates as part of the deployment package.

###### Can Mantra handle a multi-country enterprise deployment?

Yes. Mantra devices are deployed in 50+ countries across Asia, Africa, the Middle East, and Latin America. The central management platform supports multi-timezone, multi-currency, and multi-language configurations. For enterprise customers with regional headquarters in India and facilities abroad, Mantra can supply hardware to overseas locations through our global distribution network and provide remote configuration, monitoring, and software support centrally from India. For countries with specific biometric device certification requirements (e.g., national AFIS compliance in certain African markets), our international sales team can advise on the appropriate device variant for each jurisdiction.
