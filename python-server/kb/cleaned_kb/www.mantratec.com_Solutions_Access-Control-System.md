# Access Control System

Our Access Control solutions offer flexible, secure, and configurable management for campuses, enterprises, transport terminals, and high-security facilities. With facial recognition and hosted control options, we ensure only the right people access your premises after successful authentication.

## Access Built Around Roles and Responsibilities

Whether you operate a growing startup, a multi-location business, or a campus in the public sector, access management is critical to both data privacy and physical security.

*   Limit unauthorized access
*   Real time monitoring of entry/exits
*   Keep detailed access logs that can be audited
*   Improve the safety of the visitors and employees

Our systems are designed to meet today's security threats and tomorrow's operational requirements.

## Access Control Built Around You

### Standalone Access Systems

Perfect for small and medium-sized businesses with single-point entry requirements. Standalone systems are easy to deploy and operate without requiring network infrastructure. These can be upgraded later to networked models.

### Managed Access Control

With managed access control, we handle the maintenance, monitoring, and configuration of your system. Ideal for organisations without a dedicated IT/security team, this model ensures professional oversight, prompt updates, and round-the-clock reliability.

### Visitor Management Solutions

Secure your premises with real-time guest tracking, self-service registration kiosks, ID verification, and pre-approved visitor scheduling, all integrated seamlessly with your existing security systems.

### Hosted Access Cloud

Cloud-based access management lets administrators control permissions from anywhere, remove the need for on-site servers, monitor multiple locations, remotely grant or revoke access, and easily view logs and generate detailed reports.

## Frequently Asked Questions

We've got answers to your most asked questions regarding our Access Control & Security solutions.

### Can the system alert us to unauthorized visitors?

Yes. Our IPAS-AI systems feature Unregistered Person Detection. This immediately alerts your security team if an unknown user attempts to enter the premises, keeping your enterprise campus or facility secure.

### How do you prevent identity spoofing with photos or masks?

Our biometric terminals utilize advanced Anti-spoofing Technology. This ensures true physical presence and prevents unauthorized entry attempts using physical photos, digital screens, or 3D masks.

### Are your access terminals suitable for high-traffic environments?

Absolutely. We provide high-throughput turnstiles and rapid facial recognition terminals that process entries in milliseconds. This smartly and effectively prevents bottlenecks at transport terminals and large enterprise campuses.

### Do you offer cloud-based or standalone security management?

We offer flexible configurations tailored to your specific operational requirements. You can choose from Standalone Access Systems, fully Managed Access Control, or Hosted Access Cloud environments to match your infrastructure.

### What is the battery life for your portable access terminals?

Our portable devices, such as the Bio HHT, are engineered with extended battery life to ensure long operation times. This guarantees continuous, mobile security and visitor management without the need for frequent recharging.
