## FAQ

### Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our RFID security solutions.

### What is the main advantage of using RFID over traditional barcodes?

Unlike traditional barcode scanning, which requires direct line-of-sight and individual manual scans, our versatile RFID readers can capture hundreds of tags simultaneously from a distance. This eliminates manual tracking entirely, drastically accelerating workflows and reducing human error.

### How does RFID improve inventory management and loss prevention?

By implementing UHF asset tracking, you eliminate manual scanning errors, reduce labor costs, and gain complete visibility over your physical assets. Our loss prevention systems provide a real-time digital twin of your inventory, immediately alerting you to misplaced items or shrinkage.

### Can your systems manage both physical HR files and vehicle access?

Absolutely. We provide end-to-end Enterprise RFID Automation. You can digitally track sensitive HR documents within secure zones to ensure compliance, while simultaneously using UHF tags combined with ANPR (Automatic Number Plate Recognition) to automate gate operations and eliminate perimeter bottlenecks.

### Are your RFID tags and readers suitable for harsh industrial environments?

Yes. We deliver ruggedized, reliable RFID hardware specifically designed for the world's most demanding environments. Whether deployed on outdoor logistics yards, manufacturing floors, or high-traffic transport terminals, our tag and reader ecosystems ensure consistent, accurate data capture.

### How do RFID tracking systems improve overall workplace security?

RFID automation enhances security by providing real-time visibility over your most critical assets and personnel. By automatically logging every entry, exit, and asset movement, our systems restrict unauthorized access, prevent asset misplacement, and generate automated audit logs to ensure total facility compliance.
