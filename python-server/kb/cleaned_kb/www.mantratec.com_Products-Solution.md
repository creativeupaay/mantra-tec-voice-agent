## FAQ

### Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our biometric software solutions and integration capabilities.

*   **What is Multi MABIS and how is it used?**
    MULTI MABIS is an Automated Biometric Identification System developed for end-to-end deployment of large-scale multi-biometric projects. It supports multi-modal biometric data and is highly scalable for national-level identification programs.

*   **How does Bio Logon enhance enterprise security?**
    Bio Logon provides secure, passwordless authentication for mobile apps and web portals using advanced fingerprint technology. It eliminates the risks associated with password management and prevents unauthorized access through sophisticated biometric verification.

*   **Is M-Authentication compliant with Digital India initiatives?**
    Yes, Mantra's M-Authentication is specifically designed to be fully compliant with Aadhaar integration and Digital India initiatives. It provides a secure platform for mobile and cloud-based identity verification for residents across the country.

*   **Can these software products be integrated with existing HRMS or ERP systems?**
    Absolutely. All our software products come with comprehensive APIs and SDKs to ensure seamless integration with your existing enterprise systems, including HRMS, ERP, and other custom management platforms.

*   **Do you provide support for cloud and on-premise deployments?**
    Yes, our solutions offer flexible deployment options. We support cloud-based, on-premise, or hybrid infrastructure environments to suit your specific business security and performance requirements.
