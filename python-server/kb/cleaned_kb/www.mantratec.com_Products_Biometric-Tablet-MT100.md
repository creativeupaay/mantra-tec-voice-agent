## Key Highlights

## The Complete Biometric Field Terminal

MT100 combines a 7" rugged tablet with STQC biometric sensors, thermal receipt printer, and full cellular connectivity - making it the definitive all-in-one device for mobile government, healthcare, and enterprise identity operations.

FAP10 | 500DPI | FBI PIV | UIDAI

### STQC Fingerprint + IRIS (Optional)

Integrated scratch-free optical FAP10 fingerprint sensor (276x336px, 500DPI, 8-bit) certified to STQC L1, FBI PIV, CE, FCC, and UIDAI standards. Optional STQC IRIS scanner for dual-modal biometric workflows requiring higher assurance.

ARM Cortex A53 2.0GHz | 2GB RAM | 32GB

### 7" IPS LCD | Android 13 | Quad-Core

7-inch IPS LCD (1024x600, 16M colors) with scratch-resistant 6H toughened glass. Powered by ARM Cortex A53 @ 2.0GHz Quad-Core, 2GB RAM, 32GB eMMC storage, and 3-Axis accelerometer with ambient light sensor for auto-brightness.

8MP AF | High-Brightness Flash LED

### Barcode + QR Scanner

8MP auto-focus camera with high-brightness flash LED doubles as a high-speed barcode and QR code scanner - eliminating the need for a separate handheld scanner and streamlining data collection workflows in the field.

70mm/sec | 48mm Print Width

### Integrated 2" Thermal Printer

Built-in 2-inch thermal printer prints at 70mm/second with 48mm effective print width in black-and-white - generating receipts, transaction records, and field reports on the spot without any external peripheral device.

Hot-Swap | DC Jack | AC 90–275V

### 8940mAh Swappable Battery

Industry-leading 8940mAh swappable Li-Ion battery powers full-day intensive field operation. Hot-swap capability enables zero-downtime continuous use across long shifts. Adaptor input 90–275V AC with surge protection, 5V/3A DC output.

2x Micro SIM | microSD 256GB | BIS | IP54

### 4G LTE | Wi-Fi ac | BT 5.1 | GPS

Full connectivity stack: 2G/3G/4G LTE, dual-band Wi-Fi 802.11 ac, Bluetooth 5.1 (16dB TX), 16-channel GPS/AGPS, 2x Micro SIM, and microSD up to 256GB. CE, RoHS, BIS, FCC, and IP54 certified for field and outdoor deployments.
