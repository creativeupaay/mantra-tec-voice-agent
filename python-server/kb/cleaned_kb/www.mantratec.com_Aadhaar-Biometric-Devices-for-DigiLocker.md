## Questions on DigiLocker Biometric Access

Common questions about using Mantra biometric devices with DigiLocker for secure digital document access.

### What is DigiLocker and how does biometric authentication work with it?

DigiLocker is a cloud-based document wallet launched by MeitY under Digital India. Citizens store and access official documents linked to their Aadhaar. Biometric authentication (via a UIDAI-approved fingerprint or iris scanner) provides the most secure sign-in - verifying identity through encrypted UIDAI authentication before granting document access.

### Are DigiLocker documents legally valid?

Yes. DigiLocker documents are legally equivalent to original physical documents under the Information Technology Act, 2000. Driving licences, RC books, Aadhaar, PAN, mark sheets and insurance policies issued through DigiLocker are accepted by government departments, courts and institutions across India.

### Why use biometric authentication instead of OTP for DigiLocker?

Biometric authentication is more secure than OTP - it cannot be intercepted, guessed or shared. In areas with poor mobile connectivity, OTP delivery can fail, making biometric the more reliable authentication method. Biometric also eliminates SIM-swap attacks and OTP phishing risks.

### Which Mantra device is recommended for DigiLocker kiosks?

Mantra MFS110 fingerprint scanner is the recommended primary device for DigiLocker authentication kiosks. For users with fingerprint difficulties, the MIS100V2 iris scanner serves as a reliable alternative. Both are UIDAI L1 certified and STQC approved for government use.
