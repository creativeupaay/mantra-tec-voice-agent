## How to Create a Support Ticket

Follow these simple steps to log your request on our dedicated portal and get rapid, expert assistance.

### 1. Visit Portal

Log on to **servico.mantratecapp.com** to access the centralized support dashboard where all requests are managed.

### 2. Select Request Type

Choose from **Software Query**, **RMA Request**, or **Installation Request** based on your specific needs.

### 3. Submit & Track

Fill in your details and submit. You'll receive an **auto-email with a ticket number** to track progress directly from your inbox.
