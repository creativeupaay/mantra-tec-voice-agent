## Tamper-proof Government Attendance with Aadhaar Biometric Authentication

The Aadhaar Enabled Biometric Attendance System (AEBAS) is a Central Government initiative mandated by the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT) under the Ministry of Personnel. It records the attendance of all Central Government employees through Aadhaar-based biometric authentication - instantly uploading attendance data to the attendance.gov.in portal managed by NIC.

Mantra's UIDAI L1 certified biometric devices - fingerprint scanners and iris scanners - are deployed at government office entry points where employees place their fingerprint or iris for authentication. The system eliminates manual attendance registers, proxy marking and buddy punching - providing government departments with real-time, accurate attendance records and analytics.

*   Centralised monitoring by DoPT

## AEBAS Attendance Process Flow

1.  **Employee Authentication**
    The employee places their finger on the Mantra biometric device installed at the office entrance. The device captures the fingerprint and authenticates via UIDAI's encrypted L1 RD Service in under 2 seconds.
2.  **Instant Attendance Record**
    On successful Aadhaar authentication, the attendance timestamp - date, time and employee identity - is logged and instantly transmitted to the attendance.gov.in portal for real-time viewing.
3.  **Centralised Monitoring**
    Department heads, ministry officials and DoPT view attendance dashboards, punctuality reports and absence analytics on attendance.gov.in - enabling data-driven HR management across government.

## Questions on AEBAS Attendance

Common questions about implementing the Aadhaar Enabled Biometric Attendance System with Mantra biometric devices.

*   **What is AEBAS and how does it work?**
    AEBAS (Aadhaar Enabled Biometric Attendance System) is a Central Government initiative where employees mark their attendance using Aadhaar biometric authentication - placing their fingerprint or iris on a UIDAI-approved device at their office. Attendance is instantly recorded and synced to attendance.gov.in for centralised monitoring by DoPT and ministry officials.
*   **Is AEBAS mandatory for all Central Government employees?**
    Yes. The DoPT has mandated Aadhaar-enabled biometric attendance for all Central Government employees under the Government of India's Digital India initiative. All Central Government ministries, departments and attached offices are required to implement AEBAS for employee attendance management.
*   **Is Mantra MFS110 compatible with attendance.gov.in?**
    Yes. Mantra MFS110 supports UIDAI L1 RD Service and is fully compatible with the attendance.gov.in AEBAS web application. The device installs via USB plug-and-play on Windows systems - no complex configuration required. IT administrators simply register the device on the portal and employees can begin marking attendance immediately.
*   **Can state governments also implement AEBAS?**
    Yes. While AEBAS was initially mandated for Central Government, many state governments have implemented their own Aadhaar-based biometric attendance systems. Mantra MFS110 and MIS100V2 are deployed across state government offices in multiple states - compatible with both the NIC attendance.gov.in system and state-level HR management applications.
*   **What is the difference between AEBAS and GPS Biometric Attendance?**
    AEBAS uses fixed biometric devices installed at office entry points - employees must be physically present at the office to mark attendance. The GPS Biometric Attendance System adds location tagging via smartphone GPS - used for field officers who work outside the office. Both systems use Aadhaar authentication; the difference is in deployment mode and location verification.
