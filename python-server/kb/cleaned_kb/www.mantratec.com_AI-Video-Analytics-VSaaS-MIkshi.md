## Going beyond security

What your cameras can tell you about your business

Security is the floor, not the ceiling. The same footage that keeps your premises safe can tell you which product display is not drawing attention, which checkout lane causes the most abandonment, and whether your cleaning crews are hitting their schedule.

*   **Know What Your Customers Actually Do**
    Heat maps and path analysis show you where people stop, what they skip, and which areas of your floor drive the most time-on-site. Use it to inform store layouts, signage placement, and staffing shifts.
*   **Catch Problems Before They Become Expensive**
    Motion rules and occupancy thresholds trigger alerts the moment something is off. Your team gets a phone notification, not a call from a customer asking why the loading dock was left open.
*   **Right-Size Your Physical Space**
    Occupancy data over time shows you which rooms, floors, or zones are carrying the load and which ones are costing you money to maintain. Makes the case for lease renegotiations or layout changes with numbers, not gut feel.
