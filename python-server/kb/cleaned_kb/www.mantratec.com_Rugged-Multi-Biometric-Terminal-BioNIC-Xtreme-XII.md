## Key Highlights

## Mil-Grade Ruggedness Meets AI Biometrics

BioNIC Xtreme XII's die-cast aluminum alloy body, IP65 water-proofing, and IK09 impact resistance combine with powerful AI facial recognition and L1 fingerprint sensing to create the most capable outdoor multi-biometric terminal in Mantra's lineup.

-10oC to +60oC Operating

### Die-Cast IP65 | IK09 Vandal-Resistant

Die-cast aluminum alloy housing achieves IP65 complete dust-tight and water-jet-proof protection alongside IK09 vandal resistance - the highest impact protection class for outdoor terminals. Handles 10-90% RH non-condensing humidity.

Multi-Point Capacitive

### 10.1-Inch IPS TFT | 800*1280

Large 10.1-inch full-view IPS TFT display at 800*1280 resolution - significantly larger than standard 5-inch terminals - enabling clear interaction for outdoor gate operators, self-service enrollment, and supervisor monitoring at outdoor checkpoints.

Dual RGB + IR | 2MP

### 50K Face | 300ms Recognition

Binocular wide-dynamic RGB+NIR camera delivers 2MP at 30FPS. 45dB/100dB HDR for clear recognition in direct sunlight. Up to 50K face database with 1:N and 1:1 matching. Mask-on recognition and liveness detection.

2.2GHz Quad-Core + NPU

### High Performance Cortex-A73

2.2GHz Quad-core ARM Cortex-A73 + 1.8GHz dual-core Cortex-A53 with ARM G52 MP4 GPU and deep learning NPU. 2GB RAM, 16GB eMMC storage, Customized Android OS and 1M event log capacity.

PoE | 4G Optional

### Universal Connectivity Suite

Power over Ethernet (PoE) for single-cable installation. Optional 4G LTE module for remote deployments. Complete interface: RJ45, Wi-Fi/BT, RS232, RS485, USB-A, Wiegand I/O, and 2*Relay.

CE | RoHS | FCC | UL

### Global Compliance & Security

Full compliance: CE, RoHS, IP65, FCC, UL, IK09, EN, and IEC certifications. Optional ISO14443 contactless + ISO 7816 contact card reader for multi-factor access. Wall-mount, pole-mount, and e-Gate compatible.
