We've got answers to your most asked questions regarding our Fingerprint Scanner solutions.

###### How does a fingerprint scanner work?

The fingerprint scanners capture an individual's fingerprint images and translate the images into a digital code based on the unique pattern of ridges and valleys, which is then used for identification and authentication.

###### Are Mantra fingerprint scanners Aadhaar enabled?

Yes, Mantra provides Aadhaar and eKYC enabled solutions that are widely used by banks, government organizations, and telecom service providers for secure identity verification.

###### What are the applications of fingerprint scanners in enterprises?

Fingerprint scanners are used for workforce management, time and attendance systems, and secure access control, often integrating with cloud-based software for remote data management.

###### Are these devices compatible with multiple operating systems?

Yes, most Mantra fingerprint scanners support Windows, Android, and Linux operating systems, ensuring broad compatibility for various project requirements.
