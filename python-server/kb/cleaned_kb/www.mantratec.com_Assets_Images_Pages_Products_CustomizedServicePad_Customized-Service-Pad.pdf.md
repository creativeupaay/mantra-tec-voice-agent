# Customized Service Pad

**Safe, Convenient & Modern Design**

## Introduction

Cutting-edge visitor verification Customized Service Pad device designed for various business security needs. With a 10.1-inch TFT LCD display and touch screen functionality, it offers a seamless user experience. The 33 LED white backlight ensures crystal-clear visibility, while the capacitive fingerprint sensor with gold coating provides secure verification. It also comes equipped with 10 USB 3.0 ports, a microphone, VGA and HDMI interfaces, and is certified by BIS and ROHS. The Customized Service Pad is designed for easy table mounting and is the perfect solution for your visitor management needs.
