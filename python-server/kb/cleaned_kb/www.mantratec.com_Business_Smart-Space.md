## How to plan a smart building access deployment.

Before enabling access automation, map entry points, visitor flows, workspace needs, hardware and integration requirements.

01

###### Audit access points

List all doors, gates, turnstiles, barriers, elevators and parking entries across your sites.

02

###### Define access policies

Map who can access which zones, at what times, with which credentials and under what conditions.

03

###### Plan visitor workflows

Design pre-registration, walk-in, host notification, badge and checkout processes for each visitor type.

04

###### Choose workspace modules

Decide on desk booking, room booking, parking and shared resource management requirements.

05

###### Select hardware

Match turnstiles, barriers, locks, readers and sensors to each access point and deployment environment.

06

###### Integrate with enterprise systems

Connect to HR, ERP, identity management and building management systems for automated data sync.
