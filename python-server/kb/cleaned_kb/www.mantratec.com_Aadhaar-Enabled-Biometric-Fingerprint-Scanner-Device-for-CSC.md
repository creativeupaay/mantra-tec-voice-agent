## Questions on CSC Biometric Devices

Everything you need to know about biometric fingerprint scanners for Common Service Centre (CSC) operations.

###### What is Apna CSC (Common Service Centre)?

Apna CSC is a government initiative under the National e-Governance Plan (NeGP) and Digital India programme. CSCs are IT-equipped service delivery points managed by Village Level Entrepreneurs (VLEs) that provide G2C and B2C services - including financial, healthcare, agricultural, educational and social welfare services - to citizens in rural India.

###### Why do CSCs need biometric fingerprint scanners?

CSC guidelines mandate Aadhaar-based eKYC verification for beneficiary on-boarding and service delivery. Biometric fingerprint authentication via UIDAI ensures the identity of applicants is verified accurately and securely - preventing fraudulent access to government services and digital India benefits.
