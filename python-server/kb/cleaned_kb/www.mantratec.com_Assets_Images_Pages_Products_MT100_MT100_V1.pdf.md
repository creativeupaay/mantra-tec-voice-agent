Introduction

Mantra MT100 is with IRIS and Fingerprint Terminal, developed based on the latest biometric technology. The use of an IRIS and fingerprint biometrics makes it more secure for any transaction and helps to increase the overall operational efficiency of the business.

The MT100 biometric terminal runs on the Android operating system and is powered by an ARM Cortex A53 @2.0 GHz quad-core processor. It also includes 2 GB of RAM and 32GB of flash memory. The device supports an external keyboard, which can be connected through a USB or Bluetooth interface. The device is designed to accommodate the rising use of biometric authentication with an STQC-certified fingerprint and IRIS sensor.
