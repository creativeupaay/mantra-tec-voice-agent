## Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our biometric attendance systems.

### Can your employee tracking software integrate with our current HRMS?

Yes. Our biometric solutions integrate seamlessly with your existing HRMS to enable smart payroll integration. This automates faultless payroll calculations and provides real-time analytics on employee hours, shift adherence, and overtime.

### Does your facial recognition technology work if employees are wearing masks?

Absolutely. Terminals like our AI-powered BioNIC F7 are optimized for mask detection using adaptive AI. They feature fast, sub-second matching speeds and utilize live-body detection to accurately identify employees and prevent photo spoofing.

### Can we manage attendance data across multiple branch locations?

Yes. Our unified cloud ecosystem centralizes data from multiple offices into a single, secure dashboard. Administrators can monitor real-time attendance, track individual employee logs, and generate custom shift reports anytime, anywhere.

### Do you offer contactless attendance solutions with temperature screening?

We do. To support modern contactless hygiene standards, devices like the MVIS80 combine rapid facial recognition with medical-grade thermal scanning. This provides health-safe, frictionless entry for your workforce without slowing down high-traffic areas.

### Are your biometric terminals compatible with government AEBAS requirements?

Yes. We provide advanced AEBAS (Aadhaar Enabled Biometric Attendance System) terminals specifically engineered to meet strict government compliance standards, ensuring secure, reliable, and standardized workforce management for public institutions.
