## Hardware-Grade Security in an Elegant Scanner

MFS110 is a fast, compact, and easy-to-handle fingerprint device offering secured biometric data, error-free and reliable identification based on unique fingerprint technology.

UIDAI | CE | FCC | IS13252

###### FBI PIV & STQC L1 Certified

Certified to FBI PIV-071006 and STQC L1 standards - the highest compliance levels for Aadhaar authentication and national identity programs. Also compliant with EN/IEC 62368-1, IS13252 Part 1, and ISO 19794-2/4.

RSA2048 | AES-GCM-256 | TEE

###### Hardware Cryptographic Security

Onboard RSA2048 key generation, AES-GCM-256 encryption, SHA-256 hashing, and TRNG. TEE (Trusted Execution Environment) support with secure memory and key-wrapping - plus secure boot to prevent firmware tampering.

276*336px | 8-Bit Grayscale | <2 sec

###### 500 DPI Optical Sensor - Auto Capture

High-resolution optical sensor at 500 DPI 2% with 256 grayscale levels captures forensic-quality fingerprints in under 2 seconds. Auto-capture with NIST quality considerations ensures consistent results across all finger types.

5V | <90mA | WHQL Driver

###### USB 2.0 Bus-Powered - Plug & Play

USB-IF compliant, bus-powered at 5V (under 90mA) - works with any standard USB host without an external power adapter. WHQL-certified Windows driver for instant recognition on Windows, Android, and Linux platforms.

61*91*54mm | 140g | Red LED

###### IP54 Rated - Scratch-Free Platen

IP54-rated enclosure protects against dust and water splashing for reliable field operation. Scratch-free sensor surface and optical platen (16.0 mm x 19.0 mm) with Red LED illumination ensures long service life.

ISO 19794-2 | ISO 19794-4

###### MINEX Compliant & Auto Detection

MINEX-compliant minutiae extractor for interoperable biometric templates. Auto finger detection with lowest FAR and FRR, supporting 180o rotation authentication. Outputs ISO 19794-2 minutiae and ISO 19794-4 image data in encrypted PID blocks.
