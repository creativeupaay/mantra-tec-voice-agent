## FAQ

### Frequently Asked Questions

We've got answers to your most asked questions regarding our biometric technologies and solutions.

### How accurate is iris recognition compared to fingerprints?

Iris recognition offers greater uniqueness. Approximately 1 in 1078 - versus fingerprint's 1 in 1045. Iris is preferred for high-security environments and populations where fingerprints may be worn, such as manual laborers or elderly individuals.

### Are Mantra biometric devices certified for government use?

Yes. Mantra devices are STQC-certified and UIDAI-approved, making them eligible for government programs including Aadhaar enrollment, PDS welfare delivery, and MGNREGS attendance management across India.

### Can biometric access control replace traditional keycards?

Absolutely - and with significant security advantages. Unlike keycards or PINs, biometric credentials cannot be shared, lost, or stolen. Mantra's access control systems support fingerprint, face, and iris-based authentication as a direct replacement for card-based systems.

### Is palm vein scanning hygienic and contactless?

Yes. Mantra's palm vein scanners authenticate without physical contact - the user simply hovers their palm over the scanner. This makes it ideal for hospitals, food processing facilities, and any environment where hygiene or PPE is a priority.

### What is liveness detection in face recognition?

Liveness detection prevents spoofing by ensuring the face presented is a real, live person - not a photograph, video, or 3D mask. Mantra's face recognition devices include built-in liveness detection algorithms to maintain the integrity of authentication even in unattended deployments.
