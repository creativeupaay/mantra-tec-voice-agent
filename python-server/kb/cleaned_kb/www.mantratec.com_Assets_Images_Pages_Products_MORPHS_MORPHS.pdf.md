# Multi Fingerprint Scanner MORPHS

MORPHS is a portable device designed to capture ten-print fingerprints quickly and accurately. It works well in various environments, including bright sunlight, dusty areas, and on dry or dirty fingers. The device captures both flat and rolled fingerprints, making it ideal for biometric verification, identification, and enrolment applications. MORPHS is certified by the FBI with Appendix F and Mobile ID FAP 60 standards, ensuring high-quality images and seamless integration with Automated Fingerprint Information Systems (AFIS). It is widely used by local and central government bodies, UID-Aadhaar, NPR, National ID projects, and international organisations like the World Bank and UNESCO. With an ergonomic design and an easy-to-integrate SDK, MORPHS is a top choice for system integrators and solution providers.
