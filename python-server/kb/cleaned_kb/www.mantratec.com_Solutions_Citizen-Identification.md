### How do your solutions prevent identity fraud and duplicate records?

We use advanced Automated Biometric Identification Systems (ABIS) for rigorous de-duplication during the identity creation phase. By cross-referencing multimodal biometrics (fingerprint, iris, and facial recognition), we ensure a strict "One Person, One ID" framework, establishing a secure Single Source of Truth for civil registries and national ID programs.

### How does biometric authentication improve Public Distribution Systems (PDS)?

Our biometric solutions are designed to stop leakage and eliminate "ghost beneficiaries" in food subsidy and welfare supply chains. By requiring secure biometric authentication at the point of service, we guarantee that government rations, funds, and resources reach the intended, verified beneficiaries without interception.

### What role does Mantra play in financial inclusion and rural banking?

We provide the technological backbone for last-mile banking. Our rugged biometric devices enable secure e-KYC compliance and support frameworks like AePS (Aadhaar Enabled Payment System). This empowers banks and microfinance institutions to safely extend branchless banking and secure transactions to the most remote rural populations.

Yes. We provide ruggedized, portable biometric enrollment kits specifically designed for challenging environments.
