FAQ

## Frequently asked questions.

Get answers to common questions about CCTV cameras, video surveillance systems, NVR solutions, monitoring capabilities, and security applications.

### What are the benefits of a CCTV surveillance system?

CCTV systems help improve security, monitor activities in real time, deter unauthorized access, support incident investigations, and provide valuable video evidence when needed.

### Which type of CCTV camera is best for my business?

The ideal camera depends on your environment and security requirements. Dome cameras are commonly used indoors, while bullet and turret cameras are suitable for outdoor and perimeter surveillance.

### Can CCTV cameras be monitored remotely?

Yes, modern IP-based surveillance systems allow authorized users to access live video feeds, recordings, and alerts remotely through supported devices and management platforms.

### What is an NVR and why is it important?

A Network Video Recorder (NVR) stores and manages video footage captured by IP cameras, enabling centralized recording, playback, video management, and secure storage.

### Which industries benefit from CCTV surveillance solutions?

CCTV systems are widely used across manufacturing facilities, corporate offices, retail stores, educational institutions, healthcare facilities, residential communities, transportation hubs, and government infrastructure.
