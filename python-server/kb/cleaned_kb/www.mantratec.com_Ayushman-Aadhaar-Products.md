## FAQ: Questions on Ayushman Bharat Biometrics

Everything you need to know about Mantra's biometric devices and their role in the Ayushman Bharat health scheme.

###### What is Ayushman Bharat Yojana (AB-PMJAY)?

Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Aarogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY), launched in September 2018, is India's largest government-sponsored health insurance scheme. It provides health coverage of up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation to approximately 10 crore poor and vulnerable families.

###### Why are biometric devices required for Ayushman Bharat?

Biometric authentication is essential to verify the genuine identity of beneficiaries at empanelled hospitals using Aadhaar-linked fingerprint or iris data. This eliminates fraud, duplicate claims, and ensures that healthcare benefits reach only genuine patients.

###### Is Mantra a recommended partner for Ayushman Bharat?

Yes. Mantra Softech is a trusted partner recommended by Ayushman Bharat Yojana for implementing biometric solutions. Our devices are UIDAI approved and have been tested with the AB-PMJAY National Health Protection Mission systems.

###### Which Mantra device is best for hospital patient verification?

The MFS110 L1 Fingerprint Scanner is widely deployed for patient verification under Ayushman Bharat as it handles diverse fingerprint conditions including elderly and manual-labour workers. The MIS100V2 Iris Scanner is recommended for patients where fingerprint capture is challenging.

###### Are the devices compatible with existing hospital IT infrastructure?

Yes. Mantra devices support Windows (x86/x64), Linux and Android operating systems and connect via standard USB 2.0, making them compatible with virtually all existing hospital management and Ayushman Bharat BIS terminal systems without additional hardware investment.

###### How does Aadhaar biometric authentication protect patient privacy?

All authentication is performed through UIDAI's secure L1 RD Service channel. Biometric data is encrypted end-to-end and only a Yes/No authentication response is returned - no raw biometric data is stored at the hospital. This fully complies with Aadhaar Act and data protection norms.
