## Compact Precision Capacitive Technology

MARC10 delivers advanced capacitive fingerprint sensing in a slick 58.1\*33.3\*12mm footprint - combining scratch-resistant surface with FBI PIV image quality.

### Advanced FAP10 Sensor

500 DPI | 256\*360px | 12.8\*18mm

Exceptional ridge detail capture from a 12.8\*18mm platen area. Delivers high-quality images even from fingers with worn, aged, or dry skin conditions, ensuring reliable enrollment and verification.

### FBI PIV & Mobile ID

IP54 & IP67 | ESD 15KV | RoHS

Guaranteed image quality that meets federal identity standards. Features an IP54-rated device shell with an IP67 water and dust protected platen area for exceptional durability in various environments.

### Fast Acquisition

Auto Finger Detection | < 2s

NIST-quality-aware auto capture completes the placement-to-template cycle in under 2 seconds. Built-in quality checking rejects poor images before they reach the backend.

### OEM Integration Ready

USB 2.0 | SPI | UART | FPC Option

Ultra-slim MOPvC10 OEM module (35\*23\*5mm) fits inside thin terminals and kiosk panels. Supports multiple protocols (USB, SPI, UART) and wire-to-board/FPC connector interfaces.

### Multi-OS & SDK Support

Windows 7/10/11 | Linux | Android

WHQL-certified drivers for Windows 7, 10 & 11, plus full support for Linux and Android. Supports MORFIN_AUTH 1:1 and MORFIN_SEARCH 1:N matching SDKs.

### High Durability Surface

7H Hardness | 5B Cross Cut

7H scratch-resistant capacitive surface maintains consistent image quality across 1 million scans. Engineered with 5B cross cut adhesion for extreme ruggedness.

## Technical Details

Full specifications for procurement, integrators, and IT managers.

### Sensor Details

**Sensor Characteristics**

*   **Fingerprint Sensor Type:** FAP10 Capacitive Sensor
*   **Pixel Resolution:** 500 DPI ± 2%
*   **Platen Size:** 12.8 mm x 18 mm
*   **Image Size:** 256 x 360 Pixels
*   **Image Grayscale:** 256 Gray Level (8-Bit Image)
*   **Max Acquisition Time:** < 2 Sec (Placement to Template)
*   **FRR & FAR:** < 0.1% & < 0.001%
*   **Device Dimension (L x W x H):** 58.1 mm x 33.3 mm x 12 mm
*   **Module Dimension (L x W x H):** 35 mm x 23 mm x 5 mm
*   **Approx. Weight:** 0.040 kg (40g)
*   **Capture Mode:** Auto Finger detection functionality & Auto capture with built-in quality check (Incorporates NIST quality considerations)
*   **Latent Finger Detection:** Supported
*   **MTBF:** 50,000 Hours
*   **Wear & Tear Cycle:** 1 Million scans
*   **Cross Cut:** 5B
*   **Surface Hardness:** 7H
*   **SALT Spray Test:** IEC 60068-2-11 / IS 9844 / ASTM G 85

### Interface

**Interface Details**

*   **Protocol:** USB 2.0, SPI (Up to 24 Mbps)\*, UART (Baud Rate: Default 9600, Up to 115200)\*
*   **Connector:** Product: USB Type-A Male, Module: Wire to Board\*\*

**Footnotes**
\* UART & SPI interface is available in sensor module only.
\*\* FPC Connector available for USB, UART & SPI interface.

### Electrical Parameters

**Electrical Characteristics**

*   **Operating Voltage:** +5.0V ± 5% (USB or UART Powered Device) / +3.3V ± 3% (Only for SPI Powered Device)
*   **Operating Current:** < 50 mA
*   **Standby Current:** < 30 mA

### Environmental

*   **Operating Temp.:** -10°C to +70°C
*   **Storage Temp.:** -20°C to +85°C
*   **Humidity (Operating/Storage):** < 90% RH, Non-Condensing

### Standards & Compliance

*   **Biometric Data Interchange Format:** ISO 19794-2 : 2005 & 2011 and ANSI 378, ISO 19794-4 : 2005 & 2011, WSQ3.1
*   **EMI / EMC Compliance:** CE & FCC Part 15B class A
*   **ESD Protection:** Contact Discharge: ± 8kV, Air Discharge: ± 15kV
*   **Safety:** EN/IEC 62368-1, IS 13252 (Part 1)
*   **Environmental Health & Safety:** RoHS Compliant
*   **Image Quality:** FBI PIV Certification, Mobile-ID FAP10
*   **IP Rating:** IP54 (Device Level), IP67 (Platen Area)
*   **Device Driver:** WHQL certified driver

### System Requirements

*   **Operating System:** Windows 7, 10 & 11, Android & Linux
*   **Standard Software/SDK:**
    *   1:1 Matching: MORFIN_AUTH
    *   1:N Matching: MORFIN_SEARCH
