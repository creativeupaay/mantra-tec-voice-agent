## FAQ

### Questions on Jeevan Pramaan

Answers to common queries about Jeevan Pramaan digital life certificates and the biometric devices that power them.

### What is Jeevan Pramaan?

Jeevan Pramaan is a biometric-based digital life certificate programme launched by the Government of India in November 2014. It provides pensioners of Central Government, State Government and other organisations with a digital life certificate through Aadhaar authentication, eliminating the need for annual physical visits to pension offices.

### How does a pensioner generate a Jeevan Pramaan certificate?

A pensioner connects a Mantra MFS110 fingerprint scanner or MIS100V2 iris scanner to a computer or Android device, opens the Jeevan Pramaan application, enters their Aadhaar number and pension details, then authenticates with a fingerprint or iris scan. On successful UIDAI authentication, a unique Pramaan ID (life certificate) is generated and shared with the disbursing agency.

### Can the Jeevan Pramaan process be done from home?

Yes. With a Mantra USB biometric device, a laptop/desktop and an internet connection, pensioners can complete the entire Jeevan Pramaan process from home. Common Service Centres (CSCs), banks and post offices also offer assisted Jeevan Pramaan services using Mantra devices.

### Which Mantra device works best for elderly pensioners?

The MFS110 fingerprint scanner handles dry, aged or worn fingerprints commonly found in elderly users. For pensioners whose fingerprints cannot be captured due to medical conditions, the MIS100V2 iris scanner provides a reliable alternative, ensuring no pensioner is excluded from the digital certificate process.

### Is the Mantra MFS110 officially approved for Jeevan Pramaan?

Yes. The Mantra MFS110 and MIS100V2 carry UIDAI L1 RD Service certification and are STQC certified, making them fully compliant and approved for Jeevan Pramaan biometric authentication across all government and private disbursing agencies in India.
