## Key Highlights

## UIDAI-Compliant Attendance Built for the Field

MBAS30 combines a certified FAP10 fingerprint sensor with all-terrain connectivity and a replaceable high-capacity battery - delivering reliable Aadhaar-based attendance from remote government offices to rural service centers.

UIDAI | FBI PIV | STQC L1

### STQC FAP10 Certified Sensor

Integrated MFS110 optical scratch-free FAP10 sensor captures at 500 DPI with 8-bit grayscale precision. Fully compliant for Aadhaar-based authentication and eKYC workflows.

2G/3G/4G LTE | Wi-Fi

### Full Connectivity Hub

4G LTE, Wi-Fi 802.11, Bluetooth 4.0 BLE, 16-channel GPS/AGPS, and Ethernet 10/100 Mbps ensures real-time attendance sync from urban offices to remote field locations.

1024*600 | 6H Glass

### 7-Inch Touchscreen Interface

7" TFT LCD with scratch-resistant 6H toughened glass and multi-point capacitive touch. Runs Android 10 for a familiar, fast interface with minimal operator training.

Li-Ion | Rechargeable

### 6000 mAh Replaceable Battery

High-capacity battery powers long field sessions. Fully replaceable design allows hot-swapping for zero-downtime continuous deployment in the field.

ARM Cortex A53

### Quad-Core Processing

1.5 GHz Quad-Core with 2GB RAM and 32GB Flash. IR-based proximity sensor automatically wakes the device when a user approaches, saving critical battery life.

CE | RoHS | BIS

### IP54 Rated Rugged Design

IP54-rated enclosure protects against dust and water splashes. Compact and portable at ~0.8 kg, ideal for mounting or carry in any government environment.
