## ABOUT BHIM AADHAAR PAY

### Card-free Payments Powered by Aadhaar Biometric Authentication

BHIM Aadhaar Pay was launched by NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) to provide a convenient, prompt digital payment mode for merchants - enabling them to accept payments from customers through Aadhaar-linked bank accounts authenticated by fingerprint or iris. The customer does not need a debit card, credit card, smartphone or internet connection - just their Aadhaar number and a biometric scan.

Mantra's L1 RD Service certified biometric devices are widely deployed at merchant POS terminals for BHIM Aadhaar Pay transactions. Our devices enable merchants in retail, grocery, petrol stations, utilities, healthcare and agriculture sectors to accept secure digital payments from any Aadhaar-linked bank account - promoting financial inclusion across rural and semi-urban India.

**Financial Inclusion Mission:** BHIM Aadhaar Pay bridges the gap for the 190 million+ Indians without smartphones or debit cards - enabling digital payments through Aadhaar biometrics at merchant points.

## PAYMENT FLOW

### How BHIM Aadhaar Pay Transaction Works

1.  **Customer Provides Aadhaar**
    The customer provides their Aadhaar number to the merchant. The merchant enters it into the BHIM Aadhaar Pay application on their smartphone or POS device.
2.  **Biometric Authentication**
    Customer places their finger on the Mantra fingerprint scanner (or looks into the iris scanner). The device authenticates via UIDAI's encrypted L1 RD Service in real time.
3.  **Payment & Confirmation**
    On successful authentication, the payment amount is debited from the customer's Aadhaar-linked bank account and credited to the merchant - both parties receive SMS confirmation instantly.

## FAQ

### Questions on BHIM Aadhaar Pay

*   **What is BHIM Aadhaar Pay?**
    BHIM Aadhaar Pay is a merchant payment interface developed by NPCI that enables merchants to accept digital payments from customers via Aadhaar biometric authentication. The customer provides their Aadhaar number and authenticates with a fingerprint or iris scan - no debit card, credit card, OTP, PIN, or smartphone needed on the customer's side.
*   **Does the customer need a smartphone for BHIM Aadhaar Pay?**
    No. The customer only needs to provide their Aadhaar number (remembered or on Aadhaar card) and authenticate with their biometric. The merchant's device (smartphone/tablet with BHIM Aadhaar Pay app and a Mantra biometric scanner) handles the entire transaction - making it accessible to feature phone users and senior citizens.
*   **Is BHIM Aadhaar Pay free for merchants?**
    Yes. BHIM Aadhaar Pay carries zero MDR (Merchant Discount Rate) - there are no per-transaction fees for merchants. The only cost is the one-time investment in a UIDAI-approved biometric device (such as Mantra MFS110) and a merchant app registration.
*   **Which banks support BHIM Aadhaar Pay?**
    BHIM Aadhaar Pay is supported by all Aadhaar-linked bank accounts across major public and private sector banks in India - including SBI, PNB, Bank of Baroda, Axis Bank, HDFC Bank and over 100 other banks that are part of the NPCI ecosystem.
