# MVYOM

## Strong Performance for Smooth Operation

Our Smart ePOS terminal is an all-in-one machine with high performance and is easy to use.

Mantra Softech is a Global Hi-Tech Manufacturer of Biometric Products and Solutions. We offer a Wide Range of Products in Biometric and RFID Industry.

## Introduction

Mantra Softech introduces the MVYOM7, an all-in-one biometric ePOS that provides robustness for Handheld and Tabletop usage for Identity authentication by fingerprint, IRIS, or Card, as well as different payment collection possibilities due to its certification.

MVYOM7 integrates front and rear cameras, a barcode camera, a smart card reader, biometric sensors, and a printer, while the ePOS device combines Aadhaar-based finger biometric and IRIS scanners, Aadhaar-based OTP authentication, and an integrated thermal printer, capable of integrating with an Electronic Weighing Scale.
