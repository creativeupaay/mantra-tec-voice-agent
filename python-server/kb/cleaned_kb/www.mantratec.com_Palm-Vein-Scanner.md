### Precision Vein Mapping

Palm vein scanner captures the image of an individual's vein pattern, reflecting it with near-infrared rays. The existing deoxidized hemoglobin in the palm vein will absorb the infra-red rays and decreases the reflection rate, which causes the veins to appear as a black pattern. The biometric vein pattern obtained is then verified against a pre-registered pattern of a vein to verify an individual.

### Integrity of Internal Biometrics

Efforts to forge an identity is remarkably challenging nowadays as the vein is an integral part of a human body and have a wealth of discriminating features that facilitates a high level of security. The sensor used in palm vein technology can only identify the biometric palm vein pattern if the deoxidized hemoglobin is dynamically flowing through an individual's vein.
