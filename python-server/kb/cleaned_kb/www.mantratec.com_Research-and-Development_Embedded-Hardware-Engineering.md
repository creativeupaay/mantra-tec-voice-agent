## Biometric Hardware Solutions

Comprehensive range of biometric hardware designed for seamless integration across embedded systems, computers, mobile devices, and smart cards.

### Fingerprint Readers

Our thermal sensor-based fingerprint readers utilize infrared vision technology to detect subtle heat flow from skin surfaces. Combining high-performance recognition with comfortable ergonomic design, these readers deliver reliable authentication across diverse applications while maintaining user comfort during extended use.

### Biometric Modules

Powerful recognition algorithm-equipped fingerprint modules compatible with multiple sensor technologies. Ideal for manufacturers and system integrators developing access control systems, time and attendance terminals, IoT devices, door locks, and mobile applications requiring seamless biometric integration.

### Fingerprint Microcontrollers

Advanced microcontrollers integrating proprietary biometric APIs specifically designed for fingerprint recognition. These solutions enable users to implement sophisticated biometric features in host systems with flexible performance options adjustable to specific operational requirements and application needs.

### Optical Sensors

Compact optical technology-based sensors capturing high-quality fingerprint images with low power consumption. Recommended for embedded devices, our proprietary algorithms reconstruct complete fingerprint images from multiple captures, ensuring accuracy in space-constrained and power-sensitive applications.
