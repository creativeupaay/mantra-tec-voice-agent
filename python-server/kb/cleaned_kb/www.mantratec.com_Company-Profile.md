## Comprehensive Capabilities

### Complete Biometric Technology Ecosystem

###### Fingerprint Authentication Tech

From compact USB scanners to integrated enrollment devices, our proprietary fingerprint sensors deliver FBI-certified accuracy with advanced liveness detection across banking, telecom, and government for secure KYC, attendance, and identity verification.

###### IRIS Recognition Systems

Dual-eye scanners with iBeta certification provide authentication for high-security applications. Mobile devices enable field enrollment while integrated terminals deliver accurate authentication with anti-spoof technology.

###### Facial Recognition & AI Solutions

NIST-listed facial recognition platforms powered by deep learning algorithms delivers adaptive learning, high-speed matching, and seamless enterprise integration for smart cities, airports, and corporate campuses.

###### RFID & Asset Tracking

Comprehensive RFID solutions through myrfid brand enable real-time asset management, inventory control, and supply chain visibility across manufacturing, logistics, retail, and healthcare operations.

###### Financial Terminals & POS Systems

Aadhaar-enabled payment devices, micro-ATMs, and biometric POS terminals power financial inclusion across rural India for banking correspondents, retailers, and government welfare disbursement programs.

###### Kiosk & Self-Service Solutions

Self-service kiosks automate visitor management, cafeteria billing, hotel check-ins, and citizen services with integrated biometric authentication in hospitality, healthcare, retail, and government offices.

###### Enterprise Access Control

Comprehensive physical security solutions including time-attendance systems, and centralized access management platforms with cloud-based monitoring and real-time alerts.

###### National ID Deployments

Specialized enrollment kits for voter ID, national identity programs, and telecom KYC projects. Complete turnkey solutions with field-ready devices across 24 states.
