## Liveness Detection with _IR Technology_

Mantra's IR-powered liveness detection ensures every authentication is from a live, present individual - not a photo, video, or 3D mask spoof attempt.

#### Passive Liveness Detection (With IR)

- IR sensors analyze skin texture, depth, and reflection
- Detects natural facial depth and authentic human features
- Best suited for attendance systems & kiosks

#### Active Liveness Detection (With IR)

- Requires user interaction (blink, smile)
- Detects sophisticated spoofing (3D masks)
- Ideal for secure access control & eKYC

## Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers regarding our secure enterprise facial recognition access solutions.

###### Can this biometric attendance system integrate with our existing payroll software?

Yes. We offer seamless native integrations with your existing HR and IT stack. Our solutions connect effortlessly with major enterprise platforms like SAP, Oracle, and Zoho to completely automate enterprise workforce management.

###### Is facial data stored as images or encrypted templates?

To ensure global compliance and strict biometric data privacy, facial data is mathematically converted into securely encrypted templates rather than stored as actual images. Your enterprise data remains entirely uncompromised.

###### What is the difference between active and passive liveness detection?

Active liveness requires users to physically blink or move. In contrast, our AI Anti-Spoofing (Passive Liveness) technology seamlessly detects true physical presence in the background without user effort, instantly preventing spoofing via photos or screens.

###### What hosting options are available for your enterprise access solutions?

We offer flexible deployment models including On-Premise, Cloud, and Hybrid Hosting. Whether you require the absolute data control of on-premise servers or the multi-site agility of the cloud, our architecture adapts to your IT policies.

###### How do your solutions solve buddy punching and slow shift changes?

By using lightning-fast 1:N face matching that processes verifications in under one second, our heavy-duty terminals eradicate buddy punching and eliminate entry bottlenecks, ensuring maximum throughput during busy shift changes.
