### Neural Processing

### 5 TOPS NPU

Powered by 2.2GHz Quad-core ARM Cortex-A73 & 1.8GHz Dual-core Cortex-A53 processor with 5 TOPS NPU for lightning-fast on-device inference and matching.

### Binocular HDR

### Dual RGB + NIR Sensor

Binocular camera supporting 45dB Linear & 100dB HDR for various challenging light environments.

### Unified Cabling

### PoE & USB OTG

Power over Ethernet simplifies installation. USB OTG support adds modular peripheral connectivity for diverse hardware needs.

### Two-Factor Auth

### Dual Smart Card

Supports optional contactless card (ISO14443 Type A/B, Mifare Classic/Desfire) and contact card slot (ISO 7816 A/B/C) for multi-factor authentication.

### High Scale

### 50,000 Face DB

Massive local database supporting 50K users with sub-second 1:N and 1:1 matching. Supports mask detection and anti-spoofing liveness checks.

### Outdoor Ready

### IP65 & 8" IPS Display

Dust-tight and water-jet proof housing. 8-inch full-view 800x1280 IPS display with toughened capacitive touch glass.
