## Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Mantra biometric solutions across all industry verticals.

### Which biometric modality - fingerprint, iris, or face - is best for my industry?

The right modality depends on your specific use case and user population. Fingerprint is the most cost-effective and widely deployed option - ideal for most enterprise and government use cases. Iris recognition is preferred where hygiene is critical (healthcare), where users have physically demanding jobs that damage fingerprints (agriculture, manual labour), or where higher accuracy is required at unmanned kiosks. Face recognition is best where hands-free operation is needed (PPE environments, high-throughput gates) or where users object to touch-based verification. Most Mantra deployments use a combination - fingerprint as primary with face recognition as fallback - to ensure universal coverage.

### Are Mantra devices compliant with UIDAI requirements for Aadhaar authentication?

Yes. Mantra fingerprint scanners and iris devices hold UIDAI L0 and L1 certification - the mandatory standard for devices used in Aadhaar-based authentication and e-KYC. All relevant devices are listed on the UIDAI approved device registry and are STQC tested. Mantra is one of India's leading UIDAI-certified biometric device manufacturers with extensive experience in Aadhaar-linked government program deployments across multiple states. Our devices are also compliant with BIS standards and carry the required BIS certification for regulated product categories.
