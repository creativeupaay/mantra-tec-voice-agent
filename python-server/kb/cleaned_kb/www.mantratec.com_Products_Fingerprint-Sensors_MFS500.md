## Key Highlights

## Reliable Optical Fingerprint Authentication

MFS500's optical scratch-free sensor delivers consistent 500 DPI fingerprint images - built for federal identity verification standards and secure identity management.

### High Precision Sensor

Optical scratch-free sensor (MOPv2.0) captures 296 x 354 Pixels images at 500 DPI 2% from a 16.4 mm x 20.0 mm platen - delivering high image quality even for dry fingerprints.

### Federal Standard Quality

FBI PIV Certification (Mobile-ID FAP10) ensures captured prints meet federal standards. WHQL certified driver with CE & FCC Part 15 B class A and BIS IS 13252 compliance.

### Fast Auto Capture

Auto finger detection functionality & auto capture with built-in quality check (incorporates NIST quality considerations) completes acquisition in less than 2 seconds.

### Industrial Grade Build

IP54 dust and water protection for reliable operation. Compact 0.123 Kg device (Module: 0.025 Kg) powered via 5V +/- 5% USB. Available as OEM module (61 x 23 x 28.5 mm).

### Multi-OS Support

Supports Windows x86 & x64, Linux, and Android. Biometric data interchange in ISO 19794-2, ISO 19794-4, ANSI 378, WSQ3.1, and JPEG2000 formats.

### Easy Integration

WHQL Certified driver with Unique Serial Number enables easy integration. Features a 0 to 50 °C operating and storage temperature range.
