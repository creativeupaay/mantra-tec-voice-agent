## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does an iris scanner work?

The iris scanner captures high-resolution images of human irises and compares them with pre-existing patterns in the database using advanced pattern recognition technology for accurate identification.

### Is iris recognition technology secure?

Yes, iris recognition is regarded as one of the most trusted and secure biometric methods. It is reliable and error-free because iris patterns remain stable throughout an individual's lifetime.

### What are the common applications of iris scanners?

Iris scanners are widely used for facility access control, airport immigration (like Digiyatra), healthcare sectors, border control, secure device unlocking, and various government identification schemes.
