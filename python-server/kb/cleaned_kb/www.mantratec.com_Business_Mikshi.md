## FAQ

### Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers for teams evaluating AI video analytics, intelligent surveillance, VLM and CCTV intelligence.

##### What makes Mikshi different from traditional CCTV?

Traditional CCTV mainly records events for review. Mikshi AI adds real-time insights, alerts and analytics so teams can detect, respond to and prevent incidents proactively.

##### What infrastructure is required?

The platform works with standard IP cameras and a network connection. It can be deployed on-premise, in the cloud or in a hybrid model.

##### Can Mikshi be customized by industry?

Yes. Mikshi AI is designed to adapt to manufacturing, retail, education, healthcare, BFSI, logistics, smart city and corporate use cases with industry-specific models and dashboards.

##### Does Mikshi support real-time alerts?

Yes. The system generates alerts for predefined events such as unauthorized access, PPE non-compliance, restricted area entry, speed violations and more.

##### Can reports be generated for compliance and audits?

Yes. The platform can generate daily, weekly or monthly reports for audits, safety compliance and operational insights.
